FEATURED PROJECT · 2026

FPGA Transformer
Accelerator (TPU)

A TPU-style accelerator on a Basys 3 FPGA, offloading transformer attention over UART.

  • 8 × 8 systolic array with 64 MAC processing elements.
  • Double-buffered tiling for matrices up to 64 × 64.
  • UVM verification against a golden reference model.
  • Divider-free softmax with <0.9% mean error.
